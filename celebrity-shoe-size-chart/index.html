Shoe Size Systems Around the World

Different regions use different shoe sizing systems. The most common include:

  • US sizing — Common in the United States and Canada
  • UK sizing — Used primarily in the United Kingdom
  • EU sizing — Used across most European countries
  • CM / Mondopoint — Based on foot length in centimeters

Although these systems measure the same foot length, the numbering system differs between regions. Understanding these differences helps when comparing shoe sizes internationally.
